### 3. Capital Markets Risk & Capital Loss Warning
Investments in equity shares and corporate actions (including share buybacks and tender offers) carry inherent market risks:
- **Price Volatility:** The market price of unaccepted shares may decline significantly after the record date or settlement date, which can result in capital loss.
- **Variable Acceptance Ratios:** Acceptance ratios are estimates based on historical participation patterns and are **not guaranteed**. Actual acceptance ratios depend entirely on the number of shares validly tendered by other eligible shareholders.
- **Taxation Changes:** Buyback taxation is subject to ongoing statutory amendments under the Income Tax Act, 1961 (including Section 2(22)(f) enacted under the Finance Act, 2024). Individual tax outcomes vary based on individual tax slabs and available capital loss setoffs.

### 4. Mandatory Due Diligence
Users must verify all corporate announcements, offer prices, record dates, entitlement ratios, and tender timelines directly from the official disclosures on the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE), Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), and the SEBI Letter of Offer (LOF) filed by the respective company and its Lead Merchant Banker before making any financial decisions. Consult a qualified, SEBI-registered financial adviser and chartered accountant before trading or investing.
