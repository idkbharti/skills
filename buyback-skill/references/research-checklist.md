# Buyback Research Checklist

For every company selected for a **Deep Dive**, the AI must collect and verify all data points in this checklist before producing the report.

---

## 1. Deal Terms & Route Classification
- [ ] **Official Company Name & Ticker**: NSE / BSE symbol and ISIN code.
- [ ] **Offer Route**:
  - *Tender Offer via Stock Exchange Mechanism* (Eligible for 15% retail reservation & fixed pricing)
  - *Open Market through Stock Exchange* (Company buys via brokers up to ceiling price)
- [ ] **Current Lifecycle Stage**: Board Approved | Record Date Announced | Tender Window Open | Closed.

---

## 2. Pricing & Arbitrage Parameters
- [ ] **Buyback Offer Price (₹)**: Confirmed per-share price from official PA / LOF.
- [ ] **Current Market Price (CMP) (₹)**: Latest traded closing price on NSE/BSE.
- [ ] **Gross Premium (%)**:
  $$\text{Premium } = \frac{\text{Offer Price} - \text{CMP}}{\text{CMP}} \times 100$$
- [ ] **Face Value (₹)**: Equity share face value.

---

## 3. SEBI Retail 15% Quota Mechanics
- [ ] **Total Buyback Size**: Total cash commitment (₹ Cr) and total shares to be repurchased.
- [ ] **Equity Impact**: % of total paid-up equity shares being extinguished.
- [ ] **Small Shareholder (Retail) 15% Quota**:
  - Retail Reservation Size in ₹ Cr (`Total Size × 15%`)
  - Total Shares reserved exclusively for Small Shareholders (`Total Shares × 15%`)
- [ ] **Maximum Eligible Retail Shares**:
  $$\text{Max Retail Shares} = \left\lfloor \frac{2,00,000}{\text{Closing Price on Record Date}} \right\rfloor$$
- [ ] **Base Entitlement Ratio (ER)**: Official guaranteed tender ratio stated in the Letter of Offer (e.g. *X shares for every Y shares held*).
- [ ] **Estimated Acceptance Ratio (AR)**: Modeled final acceptance percentage based on non-participation of retail float and fractional lot adjustments.

---

## 4. Fundamental Health & Balance Sheet Check
- [ ] **Liquid Cash & Equivalents (₹ Cr)**: Cash balance vs. total buyback cash commitment.
- [ ] **Debt-to-Equity Ratio**: Confirmed leverage position (must not exceed 1.5x).
- [ ] **Profitability & ROE**: Latest 3-year Revenue, Profit After Tax (PAT), and Return on Equity (ROE).
- [ ] **EPS Accretion**: Pre-buyback EPS vs. projected post-buyback EPS (fewer shares = higher future EPS).
- [ ] **P/E vs. Sector P/E**: Valuation comparison against listed peers.

---

## 5. Promoter Intent & Governance
- [ ] **Promoter Holding (%)**: Pre-buyback promoter and promoter-group equity percentage.
- [ ] **Promoter Participation Intent**:
  - *Abstaining*: Promoters do not tender (bullish signal).
  - *Proportionate*: Promoters tender exact entitlement (neutral).
  - *Over-Tendering*: Promoters exiting disproportionately (bearish).
- [ ] **Projected Post-Buyback Promoter Stake (%)**.

---

## 6. Official Timetable & Filings
- [ ] **Board Approval Date**: Date of board resolution.
- [ ] **Buy-by Date**: Last date to buy in secondary market to appear as registered holder on Record Date (T-1 settlement rule).
- [ ] **Record Date**: Eligibility cutoff date.
- [ ] **Tender Offer Open Date**: Date online bidding opens via broker ASBA / tender portal.
- [ ] **Tender Offer Close Date**: Bidding window cutoff.
- [ ] **Settlement Date**: Payout date for accepted shares and return of unaccepted shares to Demat.
- [ ] **Registrar Details**: Name, email, telephone, and online portal for tender verification.
- [ ] **Filings Links**: Direct URLs to Public Announcement (PA) and Letter of Offer (LOF) on BSE/NSE/SEBI.
